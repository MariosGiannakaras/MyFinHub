## Risks, ownership and one-batch correction policy

- **Confirmed QA/feedback failure classes now tracked:** screenshot premature paint (I23) + wrong import alert semantics (I18/I20) + unhandled post-save local receipt cleanup rejection (I19). These share accurate **success/failure provenance** principles, and need **bounded shared-owner fixes** rather than one-shot CSS.
- **No demonstrably lost transaction/receipt** from the inspected artifacts. Evidence for partial-store errors is source-based and needs injected negative-path tests before claiming a user-visible history/duplication problem.
- **Finance account source of truth remains authoritative**: receipt OCR or local IndexedDB never creates spending automatically without confirmed Quick Entry; card vault secrets never belong in normal finance JSON or app logs.
